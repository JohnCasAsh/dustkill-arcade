How it works

Each press flips the lamp you touch and the lamps directly above, below, left and right of it. Pressing a lamp twice cancels out, and the order of your presses never matters.

Par is the fewest presses that clear the board. Only one in four random 5×5 boards can be cleared at all, so every puzzle here is made by pressing lamps on a dark grid. That guarantees a solution.

This is the Lights Out puzzle, first sold as a handheld game by Tiger Electronics in 1995.

Chasing the lights

Work from the top row down: for every lit lamp, press the lamp directly below it. When only the bottom row is still lit, find its pattern here, press the marked lamps in the top row, and chase down once more. The board goes dark.
